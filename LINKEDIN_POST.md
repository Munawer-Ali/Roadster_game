Remember F1 Race on those old keypad phones? 📱

The one with the button that made your car fly over the other cars. I played it for hours as a kid.

When Tesla showed the new Roadster and said it could fly, that game came straight back to me. So I rebuilt it in 3D. 🏎️

You fly the new Roadster over piles of parked Teslas. Hold FLY to rise, steer in mid-air, and barrel roll for bonus points. Every move is powered by thrusters, from the lift-off to the roll and the counter-burst that stops it.

Built with Flutter, flutter_scene and Claude.

My take on AI code: it's okay to build with AI, but you need to understand how it works.
